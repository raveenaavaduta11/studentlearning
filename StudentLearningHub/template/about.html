About Us

Discover the story behind Student Learning Hub and our mission to empower learners.

Our Mission

Student Learning Hub gives students one organized place to share, discover, and manage academic resources. The project focuses on making useful notes, code, presentations, and project files easier to find.

Our Vision

The platform is designed around a simple workflow: browse by category, search for a resource, preview or download it after login, and contribute useful material through a student account.

Why Choose Us?

01

02

Searchable Resources

Categories

Organize resources into practical programs, projects, seminars, coding, interviews, and more.

04

Account Controls

Passwords are hashed, forms use CSRF protection, and protected actions require login.

05

Preview & Download

Logged-in users can preview supported files and download other permitted resources.

06

Admin Moderation

Administrators can review resources, view platform statistics, and manage non-admin users.

Project Scope

This application is a local academic prototype built with Flask, SQLite, SQLAlchemy, WTForms, JavaScript, and CSS. It demonstrates the complete resource-sharing workflow and can be extended with email notifications, stronger file inspection, and production deployment controls.
